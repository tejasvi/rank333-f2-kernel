# Kernel-only Lean proof that the rank of 3×3 matrix multiplication over F2 is 23

This repository contains a Lean 4 proof of `Top.RankGe23`: every decomposition of the 3×3 matrix multiplication tensor over F2 into rank-one terms has at least 23 terms. Every certificate in it is checked by the Lean kernel. The proof uses no `native_decide`, no compiled code and no axioms beyond `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

The final theorem is `R22K.Final.rank_ge_23` (THEOREM.md). With Laderman's decomposition it gives rank exactly 23 (`R22K.Final.rank_eq_23`).

The mathematics is in the paper. This repository is its machine-checked companion. It reuses the frozen Lean development of the native release (lean/TOOLCHAIN.md) and replaces every natively evaluated fact of that release with kernel-checked modules.

## Structure of the proof

The proof follows the paper. The final theorem combines these tiers. Each tier ends in one published theorem, and THEOREM.md lists the binders.

| tier | what it proves | how it is checked |
|---|---|---|
| point lemma | the rank bound of the quotient by a point | E1 enumeration chains; each leaf certificate is a kernel `decide` (`KJ.aggptc.point`) |
| class raises | improved lower bounds for 21 classes of subspaces | E1 enumeration chains over the class's root jobs; leaves too large for a job module go to the leaf tier (`KC.h<c>.classLB` with `NDX.h<c>.x<c>`) |
| context caps 436 and 422 | the capacity bounds CC(436,3,14) and CC(422,3,14), which replace the line lemma L479 of the native release | E1 enumeration chains over the plane classes 436 and 422; oversized and satisfiable leaves go to the leaf tier (`KJ.agg436.Root`, `KJ.agg422.Root`, `PLE.c436.Ext`, `PLE.c422.Ext`) |
| leaf tier | each delegated leaf admits no completion (NoLift / NoDecomp) | per-leaf certificates, split into parts; the equal-first-factor reduction for leaves with repeated first factors (`S4.EqF`) |
| case II | the r = 22 profile search with at least 14 rank-one first factors | the search is run again to emit its items; every item is a kernel-checked record, and the items are joined into one cover of the case-II root (`KM2.Top.segs`) |
| cases I and I13 | the r = 22 profile searches with at most 12 and with exactly 13 rank-one first factors | kernel-checked items under one top each (`KMIA.Top.vI`, `KMJJ.Top.vI`) |
| crowns | the two classes whose native facts were evaluated by `native_decide` in the native release | kernel-checked block chains (`G2.Crown303.crown`, `G2.Crown321.crown`) |

### The CC route in case (II): two plane caps in place of the line cap

Case (II) concerns 22-term decompositions with at least 14 rank-one first factors. Its segment check uses a capacity table. A class S of subspaces has a lower bound lb(S) on the number of first factors outside S, so at most 22 - lb(S) first factors lie in S. The native proof raises class 479 (the 441 lines of the orbit of L0) from 20 to 21 by the line cap (paper, Theorem 5.1). The kernel route leaves class 479 at its C3eD value 20. It raises two classes of planes from 19 to 20 instead.

| cap | class | representative E | orbit | ranks on E \ {0} | statement |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CC(436,3,14) | 436 | span{e11, e12+e23, e22+e31} | 14,112 planes | 1,2,2,2,2,3,3 | at most 2 first factors in E |
| CC(422,3,14) | 422 | span{e11, e13+e22, e12+e31} | 7,056 planes | 1,2,2,2,2,3,3 | at most 2 first factors in E |

Here e_ij is a matrix unit, and orbits are under the group of order 56,448 generated by A -> PAQ (P, Q in GL3(F2)) and A -> A^T. The C3eD table already gives at most 3 first factors in each such plane (`RT.cLB RT.K3eD_Cls.CL 436 = RT.cLB RT.K3eD_Cls.CL 422 = 19`). So each cap excludes exactly 3.

The table is `L479.cLBW [(436,19),(422,19)]`. It puts classes 436 and 422 at 20, class 18 ({row 2 = 0}) at 11 (`L479.fact18`, Lemma W(a)), and every other class at its C3eD value (`L479.cLBW_436_422`). The case-(II) root `KM2.Top.segs` is checked against this table. The line cap is not used.

**Statement chain.**
- `L479.CC436.inst : L479.CCInst 436 19 14` (module `S4.CC436`) and `L479.CC422.inst : L479.CCInst 422 19 14` (`S4.CC422`). An instance `L479.CCInst c R lo` consists of:
  - a coset IP for E with `C.R = R`, whose labels are the 63 cosets of E other than E (a leaf fixes the cosets of the R first factors outside E)
  - its completion search `L479.EnumOK`
  - `L479.LeafNoExt` for every external leaf in `C.EXT`
  - the bound `R ≤ RT.cLB c`
  - two decided symmetries between E and the class representative.
- `L479.capG_of_inst` gives `L479.CapG K_c 19 14`: at least 20 first factors lie outside K_c. `L479.classFactsW_of` turns the two caps into the class facts of `cLBW L`.
- `Top.rank_ge_23_CC` (`S4.TopCC`) is the L479 route's top theorem with the line-cap hypotheses replaced by `hCC : ∀ p ∈ L, L479.CCInst p.1 p.2 14`. `Top.rank_ge_23_436_422` (`S4.Top436`) is its instance at L = [(436,19),(422,19)].
- `R22K.FinalTop.rank_ge_23` takes each hypothesis in its producer's published form. For each cap C it takes three statements:
  - the root `KJ.agg<C>.Root.root` over `KJ.agg<C>.Root.EXT` (the completion search)
  - the EXT top `PLE.c<C>.Ext.hext_of`
  - `PLE.c<C>.Sub.sub : ∀ pl ∈ KJ.agg<C>.Root.EXT, pl ∈ PLE.c<C>.Ext.EXT`.
- `R22K.FinalAll.rank_ge_23_of` adds the capacity table (`R22K.Cap.hCap`). `R22K.Final.rank_ge_23 : Top.RankGe23` applies it to the published theorems.

**External leaves.** `KJ.agg436.Root.EXT` has 392,525 entries and `KJ.agg422.Root.EXT` has 96,469. A leaf's record is the residual problem on the 64 functionals that vanish on E. The inside terms drop out there.
- The generator (`gen7pc --plane`, or `gen7pc_eqf --eqf`) walks every solution of the residual problem.
- At each solution (a SAT leaf of the walk), the lifting test `PL.ltW3` shows that no 3 inside terms at distinct nonzero points of E complete it (`L479.LiftK`). The test has two parts:
  - rank facts on the cosets T(X) + span{b_e c_e^T}, covering every 3-subset of the 7 points of E
  - guessed inside terms where no rank fact applies.
  
  Its soundness is `PL.ltW3_sound`.
- A leaf with no solution passes the same walk with no SAT leaf.
- Each leaf gives `L479.NoLiftK` (`PL.noLiftK_of_items` in `S4.SplitR`, or `PL.noLiftK_of_itemsE` in `S4.EqF`). Then `PL.leafNoExt_of_noLiftK_rec` gives `L479.LeafNoExt`.
- Modules:
  - per leaf, `PLX.c<C>.x<id>.{H,P<j>,A}`
  - per package, one chunk `PLE.c<C>.X<id>`
  - above the chunks, the slices, levels, `Ext` and `Sub` written by `mkexttop.py`.

**G2 and the class roots.** The native capacity table takes the CovG facts `g303` and `g321`. These rest on 23 `#ndbool` facts: 12 in `R1.Ext303` (s0-s9, m0, m1) and 11 in `R1.Ext321` (s0-s8, m0, m1). The kernel route uses `G2.Crown303.crown` and `G2.Crown321.crown` instead. These are kernel proofs of `HC.E1Concl E1.IP_e<c>.Em 18`. Their types equal those of the native crowns `E1NR1.Crown<c>.crown` with the CovG hypothesis removed.

The 21 raise classes (420, 450, 451, 454, 455, 456, 458, 460, 463, 464, 465, 469, 470, 471, 472, 473, 475, 476, 485, 487, 490) use `KC.h<c>.classLB`. Their external leaves `KC.h<c>.EXT` are refuted by `NDX.h<c>.x<c>`. This is `S5.NoDecomp` of each leaf record, through `PL.noDecomp_of_items` (`S4.SplitRN`) or `PL.noDecomp_of_itemsE` (`S4.EqF`).

### The equal-first-factor reduction (`S4.EqF`)

Two terms with the same first factor can exchange their (b, c) pairs, and the decomposition is unchanged. The reduced walk `PL.checkSXRE lt a0` therefore refutes only canonical decompositions.

- **Canonical form** `PL.Canon T n a0 b c`. Take every group of terms with equal first factor, except the group of a0. Within it, the keys `PL.keyP (b u) (c u) = 512 b_u + c_u` are non-decreasing in the term index u. Here a0 is the first factor of the root branch term. Its group stays out of the ordering, so the root GL3 move still normalizes that term.
- **Canonicalization** `PL.canon_exists`. Take a node where every term outside a0's group is still active. There, swaps inside the groups make any decomposition canonical. The swaps keep:
  - the node data (`Inv`, `AsgOK`)
  - the invariant Q, when Q is swap-invariant (`PL.SwapInv`)
  - every term of a0's group.
  
  The proof uses the potential `PL.phi n b c = Σ_w w · keyP (b w) (c w)`. Each swap of an inverted pair raises it (`PL.canon_step`, `PL.phi_swap`), and it is bounded (`PL.phi_le`).
- **The walk.** `PL.checkSXRE` is `PL.checkSXR` with three added checks:
  - the root branch term lies in a0's group
  - the order rule `PL.ordOK`: when the walk branches on a term t outside a0's group, every larger-index term of t's group is still unassigned
  - skip runs in the kids loop (`PL.kloopRE`). A marker stands for a run of kids that `PL.skipOK` rejects, because the candidate's key is below a key already assigned in the group. No canonical decomposition reaches such a kid (`PL.skipOK_canon`).
- **Soundness.**
  - `PL.checkSXRE_sound`: an accepted record refutes every canonical decomposition at its node.
  - `PL.leafSXRE_sound_paths`: the main record and the part statements (`PL.PathsOKRE`) together refute every decomposition with Q.
  - There are two instances. `PL.noLiftK_of_itemsE` is the plane lift (`PL.ltW3`, Q = `PL.QK`, swap-invariant by `PL.swapInv_QK`). `PL.noDecomp_of_itemsE` is the NoDecomp case (`PL.ltF`, Q = True).
  
  The conclusions are `L479.NoLiftK` and `S5.NoDecomp`, the same as for the unreduced walk, so chunks and tops do not change.
- **Drivers and generators.** The drivers are in `S4.PLDrvRE`: `#plhdr3e`, `#plpart3e` and `#plagg3e`, and `#plhdr3ne`, `#plpart3ne` and `#plagg3ne` for NoDecomp. The generators are `gen7pc_eqf --eqf` (single-threaded) and `gen7pd_eqf` (parallel).
- **What it reduces.** It reduces the number of SAT leaves of a leaf whose terms repeat a first factor.
  - Let g range over the groups other than a0's, and let m_g be the size of g. A canonical decomposition stands for up to ∏_g m_g! decompositions.
  - By default the generators take a root branch term whose first factor occurs once (`--eqf-any-root` turns this off). Every CC external leaf has at least two such terms, so a0's group is a single term and the reduction is by the full symmetry order ∏_g m_g!.
  - On four leaves both walks are on record. The SAT-leaf counts are 1,296 -> 162 (order 8), 217,728 -> 4,536 (order 48), 870,912 -> 18,144 (order 48) and 2,612,736 -> 27,216 (order 96). In each the ratio equals the symmetry order.
  - On the 3,137 c436 leaves with SAT leaves that use the reduced walk, the order lies between 2 and 768 (median 24). On the 26 such c422 leaves it lies between 4 and 24.
- **Negative control.** A generator build with `G7_EQF_BADSKIP` emits one skip run over a canonical kid. The kernel rejects its certificate.

## Module layout

The proof is assembled with the Lean 4.30 module system. The layout keeps the final environment small and lets every module be audited where it is built.

- **Leaf units.** Each leaf certificate is its own private declaration, added synchronously (`Elab.async false`).
  - A module holds up to about 10^4 such units.
  - Its one public theorem states the module's segment with an O(1) statement.
  - The proof combines the private theorems in a balanced tree.
- **Exposed data.** Data definitions that another module's kernel must evaluate (records, tables, paths) are added with `forceExpose`, so their values cross the module boundary.
- **Aggregation.**
  - An aggregator imports its children privately (fan-in at most 100) and publishes one theorem.
  - Nothing above an aggregator loads its children.
  - The final theorem's import closure is 17,017 modules.
- **Audit records.**
  - Every module of this release (the libraries under `lean/` and the generated packages) runs `#kax` on its public theorem at build time. This walks the module's own proofs and definition bodies, independently of Lean's axiom table.
  - The module writes a manifest (KMod `.kman`): public theorems (`KPUB`), units (`KUNITS`), audit summary (`KAXSUM`: bad constants, local axioms), axioms (`KAXP`), and external obligations (`OBL`).
  - A module's private part is deleted only after its build and audit pass.
- **External-leaf lists.** A module may use another module's `NS.EXT` list only if `NS.EXT` occurs in the statement of one of that module's public theorems. This is checked in Lean.
- **One name, one content.** Every module name has exactly one content in the build universe. Changed copies of shared modules carry new package names (for example `KDK`, `E1PQ`, `HCapK`).
- **Import-hash records.** Every build records the hash of each `.olean` (and, for `import all`, each `.olean.private`) it imported. The records are checked against the producers of the final set (VERIFICATION.md).

## Verification in brief

VERIFICATION.md gives every check with its artifact. In summary:

- **The final set:** 150,678 packages in the dependency graph of the final theorem, with 14,185,917 modules. Every package passed its audit:
  - the per-module records;
  - the walk from the package's public theorems;
  - every obligation into a module of the final set naming a public theorem.
- **Import hashes:** every import recorded at build time resolves to the producing package of the final set with the same hash.
- **The final audit:** a walk from `R22K.Final` and a private-level walk over all external obligations (5,008,518 entries) find only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. So do `#print axioms` and a name scan of every module.
- **Independent re-check:** the seeded 0.1% private sample was replayed by the toolchain's `leanchecker`, private bodies included.
  - All 9,538 sampled private parts: 5,914 pass, 0 fail, 3,258 not checkable, 366 in packages that were replaced.
  - The 9,165 in packages of the final set: 5,912 pass, 0 fail, 3,253 not checkable. A part is not checkable when its module imports a sibling with `import all` whose private part is not archived.
  - The sample covers the E1, class and EXT tiers. The case-II build deletes private parts before the sample is drawn, so cases II, I and I13 are not in it (VERIFICATION.md).
- **Re-check of cases II, I and I13 by regeneration** (pre-registered; VERIFICATION.md):
  - 24 packages chosen by a fixed seed were regenerated from this release with every private part kept, 6,433 modules in all.
  - `leanchecker`: 6,390 pass, 0 fail. The other 43 are aggregation modules of large packages that `leanchecker` cannot load; all 43 pass a kernel replay at the exported level.
  - All 24 package tops have the production statement.
  - 6 packages reproduce the production modules byte for byte. The other 18 re-derive the same top statement with their own item split.
- **Edited sources:** 22 library modules ship with edited comments and docstrings. Their public files equal the production files, and a constant-by-constant comparison finds every constant identical, with only docstrings differing (VERIFICATION.md).
- **Loading the theorem:** the public `.olean` files of the whole import closure are available (Mathlib's cache and two archives of the Zenodo record). `lean/final/check/check_closure.sh` loads `R22K.Final` without building anything and prints its statement and axioms in a few seconds (THEOREM.md).
- **Full replay:** the private parts of the other modules are not archived. A full independent replay regenerates them (REGENERATE.md).

**Compute:** the module builds of the final set took about 530,000 vCPU-hours (529,504 CPU-hours of elaboration and kernel checking, summed over all modules).
